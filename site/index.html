How it works

1

Calibrate

Sit up straight for five seconds. That's your baseline.

2

Work normally

Once a second, TurtleNeck checks where your face sits in the webcam frame. Nothing leaves your computer.

3

Get roasted

Drop your head or creep toward the screen for 5+ seconds and the turtle slides in.

Everything a posture nag needs

🔥

Escalating sass

Gentle at first, then annoyed, then furious. Write your own lines, or swap the turtle for a cat, owl, or your own photo.

🔒

Private by design

Face detection runs on your machine. No video is recorded, stored or uploaded. No accounts, no analytics.

📊

Stats & streaks

Daily score, a GitHub-style year calendar, and streaks that don't break just because you took the weekend off.

☕

Break reminder

A Pomodoro-style timer that tells you to stand up and stretch, and remembers your settings.

🎯

Stays out of the way

Work-hours schedule, pause per app, sensitivity and cooldown controls. One click in the menu bar to pause.

🪶

Light on battery

Analyzes one frame per second. Lives quietly in your menu bar or system tray.

Install in one line

Or just download the DMG. Run the same command again to update.

In Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kpryu6/turtleneck/main/install.sh | bash

With Homebrew:

brew tap kpryu6/turtleneck && brew trust kpryu6/turtleneck && brew install --cask turtleneck

Download TurtleNeck-Setup.exe

Or in PowerShell:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kpryu6/turtleneck/main/install.ps1 | iex

Installs for your user only, no admin rights needed. Uninstall any time from Settings → Apps. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the app isn't code-signed yet: choose More info → Run anyway.

Built in the open

Every release is built from source by GitHub Actions and ships with signed build provenance, so you can check that the file you downloaded came from this repository:

gh attestation verify TurtleNeck.dmg --repo kpryu6/turtleneck

The camera feed never leaves your device. TurtleNeck only keeps the position and size of your face to compare against your baseline.
